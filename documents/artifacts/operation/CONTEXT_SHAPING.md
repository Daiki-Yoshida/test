# Context Shaping

Read this when writing instructions, task specs, reviews, handoffs, or explanations that should guide a consumer toward a particular understanding, decision, or action.

## Start from the needed model

Shape context around the consumer's purpose.

State the needed model, decision, or procedure directly, then include the conditions, exceptions, rationale, and constraints required to apply it correctly.

This is **positive-first context shaping**. It is not a rule that positive wording or the selected model must literally appear first.

If the decision is unresolved, state the purpose, known conditions, candidates, evidence needs, and unresolved points directly. Do not invent certainty to make the guidance look positive-first.

## Negative Alternative Leakage

**Negative Alternative Leakage (NAL)** occurs when context introduces, repeats, or expands a non-selected or undesired alternative mainly to negate it, beyond what the consumer needs for the current purpose.

A useful review question is:

> If this alternative description were removed or shortened while the necessary model, rationale, constraints, safety, and verification information remained, would the consumer lose anything needed for the task?

If not, the extra alternative context is likely leakage.

NAL can occur before any wrong answer or implementation is observed. It is a property of how the context is shaped.

## When alternatives belong

Keep alternatives when they carry necessary value for the purpose, including:

- comparison or selection;
- correction or review;
- migration or legacy compatibility;
- incident or failure analysis;
- necessary teaching contrast or counterexamples;
- history, provenance, or decision lineage.

Judge the role of the information, not the document type.

## Negative guards

NAL does not ban negative statements.

Keep active prohibitions, safety/security constraints, ownership rules, and short guards for realistic failure modes when they are needed for correct decisions.

Do not weaken normative strength, conditions, exceptions, or safety to make guidance "positive."

When a short guard is sufficient, do not expand the full alternative model merely to explain the prohibition.

## Preservation boundary

Context shaping controls what a consumer needs now. It does not authorize deletion of source evidence, rejected or superseded knowledge, unresolved conflicts, or decision history from systems whose responsibility is preservation.
